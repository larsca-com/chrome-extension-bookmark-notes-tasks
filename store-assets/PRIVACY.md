# Privacy Policy - Bookmark Notes & Tasks

_Last updated: 2026-07-10_

Bookmark Notes & Tasks ("the extension") is designed to keep your data on your
own device. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with
your information.

## Data the extension stores

The extension stores the following locally in your browser using the Chrome
`storage.local` API:

- Bookmarks you create (page title and URL)
- Notes you write
- Tasks you add and their completed / pending state

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, synced to a remote
server, or shared with the developer or any third party. Removing a bookmark, or
uninstalling the extension, deletes the associated data from your browser.

## Data the extension does NOT collect

- No account, sign-up, or login.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No selling or transfer of user data to third parties.
- No use of your data for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

## Third-party requests

None. Site icons shown next to your bookmarks are provided by Chrome's built-in
favicon API, which reads icons already cached by your browser. All fonts are
bundled inside the extension. Nothing is loaded from an external server, so the
extension works fully offline.

## Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions it needs to function: `storage` (to
save notes and tasks locally), `bookmarks` (to create and manage the bookmarks
your notes and tasks attach to), `tabs` (to read the current page's title and
URL when you bookmark it), and `favicon` (to show the site icon next to each
bookmark using locally cached icons).
